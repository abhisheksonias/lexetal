# Requirements Document

## Introduction

This feature involves transforming the existing dark-themed hero section into a bright, modern design while maintaining all existing functionality and adding enhanced hover effects. The transformation should create a more vibrant and welcoming user experience while preserving the professional appearance suitable for a legal and tax services website.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1

**User Story:** As a website visitor, I want to see a bright and welcoming hero section, so that I feel more comfortable and engaged when considering legal and tax services.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the page loads THEN the hero section SHALL display with a bright background instead of the current dark gradient
2. WHEN viewing the hero section THEN all text SHALL be readable with appropriate contrast against the bright background
3. WHEN the page loads THEN the overall visual hierarchy SHALL be maintained with proper emphasis on key elements

### Requirement 2

**User Story:** As a website visitor, I want interactive elements to respond to my hover actions, so that I can clearly understand what is clickable and get visual feedback.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN I hover over buttons THEN they SHALL display smooth transition effects with color and scale changes
2. WHEN I hover over the consultation form THEN it SHALL provide subtle visual feedback indicating interactivity
3. WHEN I hover over statistics cards THEN they SHALL display enhanced visual effects to draw attention
4. WHEN I move my cursor away from interactive elements THEN they SHALL smoothly return to their original state

### Requirement 3

**User Story:** As a website visitor, I want the consultation form to be visually appealing and easy to use, so that I'm encouraged to submit my inquiry.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN viewing the consultation form THEN it SHALL have a bright, clean appearance that matches the new theme
2. WHEN I focus on form inputs THEN they SHALL display clear visual feedback with appropriate focus states
3. WHEN the form is displayed THEN all input fields SHALL be easily readable with proper contrast
4. WHEN I interact with the form THEN all elements SHALL maintain accessibility standards

### Requirement 4

**User Story:** As a website visitor, I want the statistics section to be visually prominent, so that I can quickly understand the company's credentials and experience.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN viewing the statistics THEN they SHALL be displayed with enhanced visual styling that fits the bright theme
2. WHEN I hover over statistics cards THEN they SHALL display engaging hover effects
3. WHEN the statistics are displayed THEN they SHALL maintain clear readability and visual hierarchy
4. WHEN viewing on different screen sizes THEN the statistics SHALL remain properly formatted and accessible
